# NavGate Design Direction

NavGate is a premium AI travel companion.

The landing page should feel:
- editorial
- sophisticated
- calm
- intelligent
- exploratory
- premium
- intentional

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Colors:
- warm ivory / paper background
- deep navy
- restrained NavGate gold
- dark editorial typography

Typography:
- elegant serif display typography
- refined sans-serif body typography
- generous line-height
- strong hierarchy

SIGNATURE ELEMENTS

- compass/navigation motif
- subtle travel routes
- geographic coordinates
- restrained particles
- Navie AI companion
- real product screenshots
- generous whitespace

MOTION

Motion should feel like travel.

Prefer:
- smooth movement
- subtle reveals
- elegant transitions
- directional movement
- slow atmospheric effects
- purposeful interaction

Avoid:
- excessive bouncing
- random floating cards
- excessive glassmorphism
- generic SaaS gradients
- neon AI aesthetics
- unnecessary 3D effects
- excessive animations
- animation for decoration alone

IMPORTANT

Every new interaction must have a purpose.

Do not introduce a new visual style simply because it is trendy.

Preserve the existing NavGate design system when modifying the landing page.
